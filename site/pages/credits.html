Credits

Built on excellent work.

dontv is a fork of dumbtv by @humblemuzzu. Its snapshots, full restore, never-disable lists, device catalog and launcher are the foundation everything here is built on. Thank you.

The projects

  • dumbtv

    by @humblemuzzu and its contributors and testers. The scripts, lists, launcher and device reports. Its site is dumbtv.org.

  • firetv-safe-debloat

    by @roy9611. Frame-rate matching, quiet home and the idea for the Windows wrappers.

  • debloatfiretv

    by @jannikloehr. The per-package research behind the package table, and the dry-run-first approach.

  • FireDebloat

    by @krishkatyal. The health check, based on its vibecheck, and the Projectivy Launcher notes.

And the projects they all stand on: personalDNSfilter by Ingo Zenz, HaGeZi's DNS blocklists, SmartTube and cleanyourtv.
