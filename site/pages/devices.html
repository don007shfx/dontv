Is yours a different model?

Run ./scripts/preflight.sh first. It reads the model and Fire OS version and shows what would change, without changing anything. Per-model notes from dumbtv's testers are in catalog/devices/.

After a run, ./scripts/contribute.sh writes a device report you can share. Android TV, Google TV, Samsung, LG and Roku are not covered; here is why, and what you can still do.
